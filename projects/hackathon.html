Energy AI Hackathon

Quarterfinalist. 48 hours to predict three years of oil production for 12 new wells.

  • Python
  • Machine learning
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Teamwork
  • Presenting

The challenge

We got production data from 71 wells and had 48 hours to predict how much oil 12 new wells would produce over their first three years.

What we built

My team and I built a machine learning workflow in Python to make the prediction. A single number hides how unsure you are, so we didn't stop there. We ran 100 probabilistic realizations and reported a range instead of one guess.

The pitch

We presented the approach and results to a panel from industry, and made it to the quarterfinals.
