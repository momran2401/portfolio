Conductive cement composites

How much conductivity can you add to cement before it gets weak? I built the test setups, ran the tests, and co-authored the paper.

  • Test fixtures
  • Wiring
  • Electrical characterization
  • Charge/discharge

Read the paper

The question

Mix carbon into cement and it starts to conduct electricity. That opens the door to smart infrastructure, like concrete that stores energy the way a supercapacitor does. The catch is that the carbon can cost you mechanical strength. Our work looked at that trade-off using nano-carbon black.

What I did

  • Built and modified the test fixtures, setups, and wiring for the electrical measurements, so the data came out clean and repeatable.
  • Ran conductivity and resistivity tests and compared mixtures against each other.
  • Ran charge/discharge cycling for the supercapacitor work and tracked stability and response across cycles.
  • Mixed and cast specimens and ran mechanical testing, so every electrical result had a strength number next to it.
  • Cleaned, plotted, and analyzed the data in Python and Excel for the team's deliverables.

S-RISE, summer 2025

In summer 2025 I was selected as an S-RISE Scholar, a competitive, university-funded research program at TAMU-CC. I spent it on these nano-carbon black composites and finished with a presentation and poster at the program showcase, pitched so both engineers and non-engineers could follow.

Not my first time with conductive concrete

Back in 2021 I was a research assistant at MIT's Concrete Sustainability Hub, preparing conductive cement mixtures and running electrical resistance and mechanical tests on the same basic question.
