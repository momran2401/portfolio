Projects / Hexacopter

FAA UAS Test Site, Texas A&M Autonomy Research Institute/Corpus Christi, TX/May to Sep 2025

Hexacopter rebuild and flight test

A decommissioned hexacopter, rebuilt and flying again with a camera and gimbal on board.

  • ArduPilot
  • UAS
  • Redundant comms
  • Power management
  • Flight testing

Starting point

The test site had a hexacopter that had been decommissioned. The job was to bring it back and make it carry a real payload: a camera on a gimbal, powered by a 7S battery.

The rebuild

I rebuilt it around ArduPilot. When a drone is carrying a camera rig, losing a link or a sensor in the air is the thing you can't afford, so I gave it redundant communications and redundant sensing. The power system was set up with safety first, not as an afterthought.

Flight test

Then I flight-tested it. It flew about 11 minutes with the 7S camera and gimbal payload on board.
