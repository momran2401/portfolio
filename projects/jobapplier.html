Job Applier

A job-application assistant that runs on my Mac. It tailors my resume, fills the forms, and doesn't submit anything until I say so.

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • SQLite
  • Playwright
  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Codex / Claude / Ollama
GitHub: momran2401/jobApplier

Why I built it

Applying for internships is the same steps over and over. Tweak the resume, write a cover letter, fill the same form on a different portal, then lose track of where you applied and who wrote back. I built something to do the repetitive part and leave the decisions to me.

What it does

  • One master profile. I feed it my resume, LaTeX source, LinkedIn export, and transcript. It proposes what to add, I review it, and I mark it verified.
  • Tailored resume. For each posting it rewrites the skills section of my LaTeX resume to fit the role. Always one page.
  • Cover letters when a posting asks for one, plus likely application questions with answers.
  • Form filling. It opens the application in its own Chrome window, fills it in, and stops at review. Nothing gets submitted until I approve it.
  • Tracking. Every application lands in a Google Sheet, and it watches my inbox for replies that need action.
  • Networking. It builds a list of people to reach out to and drafts first messages that I review and send myself.

It also gives each tailored resume a match report: how much of the posting's requirements it covers, plus an optional score from HackerRank's open-source intern rubric.

Three modes

  • Advisor: tailored resume, cover letter, and likely questions. I apply myself.
  • AI Applier: fills the form and stops at review. I approve, it submits.
  • Autonomous: submits without review, but only within strict limits. There's a daily cap, and anything it isn't sure about gets parked for me with the reason.

Rules live in code, not in the prompt

I don't take a model's word for it, so the important rules are checked in code after it answers. A networking draft has to be 15 to 65 words with exactly one question, no banned asks, and its source has to be a page the app actually read. If a draft fails, it's flagged for review instead of going out. It never invents qualifications. Stop cancels everything in progress.

How it's built

  • Backend: Python, FastAPI, SQLite, and Playwright for the browser automation.
  • Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, and Vite.
  • AI: swappable between my ChatGPT Plus login through the Codex CLI, the Claude API, or a local model through Ollama.
  • Privacy: everything stays on my machine. Data lives in a local folder, and passwords and the sheet link live in the macOS Keychain.

About 6,000 lines of Python, about 1,400 lines of TypeScript, and 104 tests.
