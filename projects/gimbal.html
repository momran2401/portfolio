Three-axis remote gimbal controller

Remote control for a three-axis gimbal over a 900 MHz link, with a small gimbal on the remote that mirrors what you command.

  • RFD900X
  • 900 MHz telemetry
  • Servos
  • Embedded
  • Documentation

What it does

This is a remote for a three-axis gimbal. Commands go out over a 900 MHz RFD900X telemetry link.

The mirror gimbal

The part I like most: the remote has its own little servo gimbal on it. Whatever orientation you command, the mirror gimbal moves to match it in real time. You can see what you're sending just by looking at your hands.

Handing it off

I documented the setup and calibration so other people at the site could use it after me.
